About Clyvara

A single-specialty firm, built on doctoral research into how security awareness and training affect SME performance.

Clyvara exists because of what doctoral research into security awareness and training in small businesses found: training programs fall short on customization, implementation, and evaluation — and leave a false sense of security behind.

Rather than selling another tool or another audit, Clyvara delivers the discipline of an enterprise security program — scoped, priced, and paced for a business that has 20 to 200 employees, not 20,000.

Clyvara stays deliberately narrow. No general technology advisory. No digital transformation consulting. Every dollar of effort goes deeper into cybersecurity — new frameworks, new sectors, new delivery models — never wider into adjacent, more commoditized markets.

Methodology

Clyvara's SHIELD Framework

A six-phase program that takes a business from unknown exposure to a managed, monitored security posture — each phase with a defined outcome and a deliverable you keep.

S
Survey Start here
A full risk assessment against your actual environment — not a generic checklist. You receive a prioritized, RAG-rated risk register. Every engagement begins here.
H
Harden
Remediation of the highest-priority findings first — technical controls, access management, and configuration fixes sequenced by actual risk reduction.
I
Instruct
Security awareness training built on the same research foundation behind Clyvara's methodology — addressing the human-error root cause directly, not a once-a-year video.
E
Enforce
Policies and controls put into force — applied, acknowledged by the team, and checked on a cadence, so the program on paper is the program in practice, with exceptions recorded and time-boxed rather than ignored.
L
Ledger
A monthly one-page dashboard that keeps your risk register current between formal reviews, so issues get caught in weeks, not discovered during a breach or an audit.
D
Defend
The SHIELD Defend Retainer — fractional CISO access and incident-readiness support for businesses that want a standing partner, not a one-time project.

What Clyvara does not do

Not penetration testing. Clyvara assesses risk and readiness; it does not attack, scan, or technically audit your systems.
Not legal advice. Clyvara is not a law firm; legal determinations belong to your counsel.
Not IT implementation — Clyvara is a governance advisory, and where the work calls for tools or configuration, your IT provider does that work.
No certification, no guarantee. Clyvara does not certify compliance, and cannot guarantee any regulator’s, insurer’s, customer’s, or court’s decision.
