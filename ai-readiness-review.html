The Problem

Ungoverned AI is already inside the business.

None of this requires a breach. It accumulates quietly, one helpful tool at a time, until somebody with standing asks how AI is governed here and there is no answer on file:

Private data into consumer tools. Staff paste client and patient information into consumer AI tools with no agreement governing what happens to it.
Permissions nobody reviewed. Approved tools get connected to email, documents, and records with access nobody checked.
Vendor terms that changed silently. Vendors add AI features to software you already run, and the data terms change without anyone reading them.
No answer on file. A cyber insurance application or a client questionnaire asks how you govern AI, and the honest answer is that nobody knows.
What the Review Covers

Five pieces, one written record.

One
The AI tool inventory. Every AI tool in use across the organization, including unsanctioned shadow AI adopted by individual staff, catalogued by who uses it and for what.

Two

You receive→The gap assessment

The findings note and fix list. The risks ranked in writing, and a prioritized fix list your team can act on, with or without Clyvara.

How It Works

Ten business days, start to findings.

1The intake call

A short call to scope the tool landscape and name the people to speak with. If the review is not the right starting point for your organization, you will hear that on this call, before any fee.

2Inventory and review

The inventory and review work follows, working from your tool list, the vendor terms, and brief staff conversations. Nothing is installed, nothing is monitored, and no tool is taken away mid-review.

3Findings and fixes

Within 10 business days of the intake call, the findings note and the prioritized fix list arrive, walked through on a closing call so you can explain every line in your own words.

The Fee

Fixed at $1,500. No hourly billing, no scope creep.

One organization, one fixed fee of $1,500 for the review: the inventory, the data-flow review, the vendor terms check, the gap assessment, and the findings note with its fix list. If the review surfaces a larger problem, it is named in the findings note and scoped separately only at your request.

What it is
A readiness review of how AI is actually used and governed in your organization, delivered personally by Clyvara’s principal consultant, measured against the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
What it is not
Not a legal opinion, and not a certification of compliance with any AI law. Texas’s AI statute (TRAIGA) binds state agencies; most small organizations face their AI duties through client contracts, insurers, and sector regulators instead, and the review is framed on those. Not an AI product, a monitoring platform, or a penetration test.
Who it fits
Small and mid-sized organizations, healthcare practices, and professional-services firms whose people already use AI tools day to day, and who would rather govern that use deliberately than discover it in a questionnaire.

Payment is by electronic invoice, payable when work starts. What you share stays with Clyvara: your tool list, documents, and staff conversations are held confidentially and shared only with you.
