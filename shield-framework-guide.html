The SHIELD Model

Six phases. One sequence.

Every Clyvara engagement moves through the same six phases, in the same order — mapped to NIST CSF 2.0 and CIS Controls v8.

S
Survey
H
Harden
I
Instruct
E
Enforce
L
Ledger
D
Defend
SSurvey

A full risk assessment against your actual environment — not a generic checklist. You receive a prioritized, RAG-rated risk register.

HHarden

The highest-priority findings addressed first, sequenced by actual risk reduction. Your IT provider makes the technical changes; Clyvara directs the order and verifies the result.

IInstruct

Security awareness training built on original research — addressing the human-error root cause directly, not a once-a-year video.

EEnforce

Policies and controls put into force — the standards set in the earlier phases are applied, acknowledged by the team, and checked on a cadence, with every exception recorded and time-boxed rather than quietly ignored.

LLedger

A monthly one-page dashboard that keeps your risk register current between formal reviews, so issues get caught in weeks, not at audit time.

DDefend

The SHIELD Defend Retainer — fractional CISO access and incident-readiness support for a standing partner, not a one-time project.
