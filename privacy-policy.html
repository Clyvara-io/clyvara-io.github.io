Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information clyvara.io collects, why, and who processes it. It is deliberately short, because the site’s data collection is deliberately small. Effective November 12, 2026.

What we collect

Contact-form submissions. When you use the contact form on this site, we receive the name, email address, and message you enter. Submissions are processed for us by Formspree, our form-handling provider.

Gated resources. Forms that offer a guide or checklist ask for an email address only, so the resource can be delivered to you.

Site usage data. This site uses Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-VC555D8ERL). Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data such as the pages you visit, how long you spend on them, and your approximate location. We see this information in aggregate reports, not as a profile of an identified individual.

How we use it

We use contact-form submissions and email addresses to respond to enquiries and to deliver the resources you requested. We use aggregate analytics to understand which pages are useful and to improve the site. That is the whole list: no advertising profiles, no marketing lists built from form data without your asking.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with anyone other than the service providers named in this policy, Formspree and Google, who process it on our behalf for the purposes described above.

Retention and your choices

Enquiries are kept as long as needed for the business relationship they start. If you would like your information deleted, email us at Info@clyvara.io and we will delete it and confirm when that is done. You can also block or clear cookies through your browser settings, which stops analytics collection on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions about this policy or about your information go to Info@clyvara.io. Clyvara LLC is a cybersecurity governance advisory based in Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas.
