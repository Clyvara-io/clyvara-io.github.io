How It Works

Four steps, days not weeks.

1Book a triage call

Thirty minutes on the questionnaire on your desk: what the sender is really testing, what evidence you already hold, and what would need creating. If the questionnaire does not fit this service’s scope, you hear that here, before any fee is due.

2Send it

You send the questionnaire and your deadline. If the date cannot be met honestly, you hear that the same day, not the day before it.

3The evidence pass

Clyvara drafts every answer against your documents and records, and flags anything that cannot be evidenced as claimed. Nothing goes in that you could not produce within a business day.

4Delivery

The completed questionnaire, the evidence index, and the gap list, plus the walkthrough call. In the standard case, delivery is within three business days of the triage call.

The Fee

Fixed at $750. No hourly meter.

One questionnaire, one fixed fee of $750, payable in full when we start. If the triage call shows the work does not fit this scope — a questionnaire running to hundreds of questions, or one that really needs a full assessment underneath it — the fee is not taken and the honest route is named instead.

If you then sign a Risk & Readiness Assessment or a SHIELD Defend Retainer within 60 days of delivery, the full $750 is credited toward that engagement.

What it is
A completed, evidence-backed response to one customer’s security questionnaire, plus the index and gap list behind it. Led and delivered personally by Clyvara’s principal consultant.
What it is not
Not a full risk assessment — that is the Risk & Readiness Assessment. Not remediation work, and not a promise about the customer’s decision. Answers stay truthful even when the truthful answer is uncomfortable; that is what makes them hold up.
Who it fits
Businesses answering customer or partner questionnaires to win or keep work — healthcare practices, professional-services firms, and the suppliers around them — where the answers matter and the evidence is scattered.

Payment is by electronic invoice when we start. What you share stays with Clyvara: your answers, evidence, and gaps are held confidentially, shared only with you, and an NDA is available on request before the triage call.
