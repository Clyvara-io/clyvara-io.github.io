Texas SB 2610: a cybersecurity program that can stand as your defense.

Since September 1, 2025, Texas law has given businesses with fewer than 250 employees an affirmative defense against exemplary (punitive) damages in data-breach litigation, when they have implemented and maintained a qualifying cybersecurity program. The defense is the program, documented and maintained. Clyvara builds it with you, together with an indexed evidence binder that helps your counsel evaluate and demonstrate the program.

What the Law Rewards

A program on paper is not enough. It has to be alive.

SB 2610 scales its requirements to the size of the business, in three tiers: under 20 employees — simplified requirements, including password policies and appropriate employee cybersecurity training; 20 to 99 employees — the CIS Controls Implementation Group 1 (IG1); 100 to 249 employees — compliance with the requirements of an industry-recognized cybersecurity framework, such as NIST CSF or ISO 27001. Clyvara builds your program to the tier that fits your headcount. The statute rewards two things at once: a program that exists in writing, and records showing it is implemented and maintained over time. Most small businesses have neither. This package produces both:

One
A written program, aligned and scaled. Your cybersecurity program documented against the requirements of your SB 2610 tier, proportionate to a business of your size: what you protect, how you protect it, who owns each part.
Two
Core policies. The governing documents the program stands on: access control, acceptable use, incident response, vendor oversight, and data handling, written for your business rather than copied from a template library.
Three
An evidence binder. The records that show the program is implemented and maintained: the risk register, review dates, training notes, and decisions, organized so your counsel can put the program in front of a court without a reconstruction project.
Four
An annual review date. Maintenance is part of the bargain the statute offers. Your first annual review is scheduled at delivery, so the program stays current instead of aging in a drawer.
