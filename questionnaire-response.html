What You Get

Answers you can defend, not answers you hope hold up.

A questionnaire is a request for evidence wearing a spreadsheet’s clothes. This service produces the response and the file behind it:

One
The completed questionnaire. Every question answered, in the format it arrived — spreadsheet, document, or portal questions returned as a completed copy — ready for you to submit.
Two
An evidence index. Each answer tied to the document or record that backs it, so when the follow-up “show me” arrives, it takes minutes to answer, not days of reconstruction.
Three
Honest gap notes. Where the truthful answer is “not yet,” the response says so, with a date and an owner — the answer reviewers trust, instead of a bare yes that collapses at audit or renewal.
Four
A gap list with fixes. The short list of what to put in place before the next questionnaire lands, in priority order, so this scramble does not repeat.
Five
A walkthrough call. Thirty minutes together over the finished answers before you send them, so you can defend every line in your own words.
How It Works

Four steps, days not weeks.

1Send it

You send the questionnaire and your deadline. If the date cannot be met honestly, you hear that the same day, not the day before it.

2Triage call

Thirty minutes on what the sender is really testing, what evidence you already hold, and what would need creating. If the questionnaire does not fit this service’s scope, you hear that here, before any fee is due.

3The evidence pass

Clyvara drafts every answer against your documents and records, and flags anything that cannot be evidenced as claimed. Nothing goes in that you could not produce within a business day.

4Delivery

The completed questionnaire, the evidence index, and the gap list, plus the walkthrough call. In the standard case, delivery is within three business days of the triage call.
